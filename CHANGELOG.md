# Changelog

## 3.0.0 — The Duel Edition

- AI-first, five-round 1v1 series with 120-second rounds, point scoring, draws, verdict screens and rotating callsigns.
- Identical manual cannon loadouts: 500 m path, eight rounds/second, four-hit kills, heat and swept physical hitboxes.
- Separate Last Stand air-to-ground challenge with a three-second-recharge manual railgun.
- Seven new original arenas: airport, military station, desert strip, downtown, carrier group, farmland and wind farm.
- Animated landing gear with additional drag; keyboard and touch controls.
- Compact cannon/lead sights, live series scoreboard, round history and mode-specific help.
- Theater-tagged colliders, shared render/collision terrain triangles, real bridge gaps and moving turbine blade collisions.
- Preserved missile Operation, Free Hunt, Flight Practice, one-second missile reload and 2.5-second flares.
- Expanded deterministic runtime tests; local/static/offline hosting remains dependency-free.

All locations and procedural assets are original. This release adds AI gameplay, not online multiplayer.
